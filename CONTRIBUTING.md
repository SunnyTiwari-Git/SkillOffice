# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in SkillOffice.

## Current policy: issues only

SkillOffice is maintained by its owner. **Pull requests are not accepted yet** and will be closed without review. This keeps every line of the library reviewed by one accountable person while the foundations are built.

## Issues are welcome

Open an issue for:

- **Bug reports:** a skill gives wrong, unsafe or inconsistent output. Include the AI tool, the request, and what you expected.
- **Skill requests:** a task your department needs that SkillOffice does not cover.
- **Platform reports:** a skill works or fails on a specific AI tool.
- **Documentation problems:** something unclear or wrong.

Do not include company secrets, credentials or personal data in issues.

Report security problems privately. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Suggesting an existing open-source skill

You can suggest a third-party skill in an issue. Include its repository URL and license. It will go through the full security review before it is considered, and it may be declined for license or safety reasons.

## Future contributions

The owner may open reviewed contributions after version 1.0. When that happens, this file will explain the process.
