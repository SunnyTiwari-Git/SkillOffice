# Localization checklist

| Item | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Dates | Use the target region's format (for example DD MMM YYYY for India, MM/DD/YYYY for the US). Spell out the month when the reader could misread day and month. |
| Times | Add the time zone; prefer 24-hour clock where the region expects it. |
| Numbers | Use the target decimal and thousands separators. Indian English may use lakh and crore grouping when the reader expects it. |
| Currency | Keep the original amount and currency. Change only the symbol position and separators to local style. Never convert between currencies without a rate the user provides. |
| Units | Convert metric and imperial when the reader needs it, keeping the original in brackets where precision matters (for example "5 km (3.1 miles)"). |
| Names | Keep company, product, brand and people's names as they are, unless the glossary says otherwise. |
| Addresses and phone numbers | Leave as written; do not reformat into another country's pattern. |
| Formality | Match the target culture's expected formality for business text, within the brand voice. |
| Idioms and humor | Replace with plain equivalents; avoid culture-specific references the reader may not share. |
| Legal, medical, safety, contractual text | Translate for understanding only and flag for a qualified human translator. |

Back-check before handing over: every number, date, name, negation and condition matches the source.
