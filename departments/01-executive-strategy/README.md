# Executive Management and Strategy

Status: **pilot, not yet platform-tested.** Planned platforms: Claude Code and Antigravity.

> **Review before it leaves the company.** Figures and forward-looking statements in anything meant for a board, investors, lenders or the public need review by finance and legal before they are shared. This department drafts and advises; leaders decide.

This department helps founders, CEOs and leadership teams think clearly and decide well: assessing where the business stands, choosing a strategy, setting goals, building the case for an investment, writing decision memos, preparing board updates, reviewing how the business is doing and running leadership meetings around decisions.

It works from information you provide. It separates evidence from assumptions, never invents market data or figures, and never sends, posts or commits the company to anything.

## How to use it

Ask the department manager, `exe-manager`, in plain words. For example:

- "Help us set strategy and OKRs for next quarter. Here is our KPI export."
- "Build a business case for hiring two more engineers."
- "Write a decision memo: build or buy our booking system."
- "Draft October's board update from these figures."
- "How did the business do this month?"
- "A competitor cut prices. How should we respond?"
- "Stress-test our annual plan before I present it."

The manager plans which skills to run, skips the ones you do not need, does one round of fixes and gives you one report.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| exe-strategy-review | Assesses your situation and lays out strategic options with trade-offs |
| exe-okr-planning | Writes measurable objectives and key results, and scores them at period end |
| exe-business-case | Builds or reviews a business case with payback and sensitivity |
| exe-decision-memo | One-page decision memo with a recommendation and what would change it |
| exe-board-update | Drafts a board or investor update and flags what needs review |
| exe-kpi-review | One-page business health check with the one thing to act on |
| exe-competitive-strategy | Company-level competitive position and moves |
| exe-meeting-brief | Executive briefing and a decisions-first meeting agenda |
| exe-plan-review | Stress-tests a strategy, plan or OKR set |

## Playbooks

| Playbook | Skills, in order |
| --- | --- |
| Annual or quarterly strategy | KPI review, strategy review, competitive strategy, OKR planning, plan review |
| OKR setting | OKR planning, plan review |
| Business case | Business case (build), business case (review), plan review |
| Decision memo | Decision memo, plan review |
| Board update | KPI review, board update, plan review |
| Monthly business review | KPI review, meeting brief |

## What it will not do

- Send, post or publish anything.
- Commit the company to investors, customers, partners or staff.
- Invent market data, figures or quotes.
- Give legal, tax or investment advice. Deal, fundraising and legal structure questions go to the Finance and Legal departments.
