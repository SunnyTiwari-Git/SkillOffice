# Board update guide

## Structure

1. Headline (three sentences)
2. Key metrics table
3. Highlights
4. Challenges and what we are doing
5. Cash and runway
6. Decisions made, decisions needed (asks of the board)
7. Outlook (forward-looking, flagged)
8. Review flags for finance and legal

## Tone

- Plain and factual. Numbers first, adjectives last.
- Same metrics, same order every period, so trends are easy to see. If a metric changes definition, say so.
- Bad news early and in proportion, with a plan.
- Short. Detail goes in an appendix the board can skip.

## Runway

- Runway (months) = cash at period end / average monthly net burn over the last three months.
- Example: cash 900,000; net burn of 70,000, 80,000 and 90,000 in the last three months gives an average of 80,000; runway = 900,000 / 80,000 = 11.25 months.
- If the company is cash-flow positive, say so instead of showing runway.

## Forward-looking statements

Phrases such as "we expect", "we will", "on track to", "forecast" and any projected figure are forward-looking. Flag each one, keep it to what management genuinely believes, and state the main assumption. Finance and legal decide the final wording.
