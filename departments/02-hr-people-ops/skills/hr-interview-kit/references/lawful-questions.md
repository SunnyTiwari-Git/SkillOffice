# Lawful, job-related interview questions

Every question should help predict how the person will do the job. Laws differ by jurisdiction; when unsure, ask HR or legal. The table shows common traps and the lawful version.

| Do not ask | Why | Ask instead, if the job needs it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "How old are you?" "When did you graduate?" | Age | "Do you meet the legal minimum age for this role?" only where a minimum age applies |
| "Are you married?" "Do you have or plan to have children?" "Are you pregnant?" | Marital, family status, pregnancy | "This role needs travel two weeks a month. Can you meet that schedule?" |
| "Where are you from originally?" "Is that your native language?" | National origin | "Are you legally able to work in [country]?" and "This role needs written [language] for customer replies. Tell me about your experience with that." |
| "What religion are you?" "Which holidays do you take?" | Religion | "This role works some weekends. Can you meet that schedule?" |
| "Do you have any disabilities or health conditions?" "How many sick days did you take?" | Disability and health | "Can you perform the essential tasks of this job, with or without reasonable adjustments?" |
| Caste, community or family background questions | Caste and social origin | Remove entirely |
| "Have you ever been arrested?" | Heavily regulated | Route to HR and legal; do not ask in interviews |
| "What is your current salary?" | Unlawful to ask in some places | "What pay range are you looking for?" |
| "Would you fit in with a young team?" | Age and culture-fit bias | "How do you like to work with a team?" |

## Question patterns that work

- **Behavioral:** "Tell me about a time you [did something the job requires]. What did you do, and what happened?"
- **Situational:** "A customer is angry about a late order and wants a refund outside policy. Walk me through how you would handle it."
- **Probes:** "What would you do differently?" "What was your part, specifically?" "How did you know it worked?"

## Scoring anchors (example, 1 to 4)

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | No relevant example, or the example shows the opposite behavior |
| 2 | Partial example, limited scope or unclear personal contribution |
| 3 | Clear example with the expected behavior and a result |
| 4 | Clear example at greater scope or complexity than the role needs, with reflection |

Write the anchors for each competency in this role's own terms.
