# Onboarding plan outline

## Pre-start (before day one)

| Item | Owner | Done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome message with start time, place or link, what to bring (draft for the manager to send) | Manager | [ ] |
| Paperwork, contract and right-to-work steps for the jurisdiction (confirm with HR) | HR | [ ] |
| Equipment ordered and delivered | IT or operations | [ ] |
| Accounts and access requested at the lowest level needed | IT | [ ] |
| Team calendar and recurring meetings | Manager | [ ] |
| Onboarding buddy chosen (not the manager) | Manager | [ ] |
| Adjustments the person asked for are in place | HR and manager | [ ] |

## Day one (example, adjust to time zone and setup)

| Time | Activity | With |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start | Welcome and plan for the week | Manager |
| +1 hour | Equipment and account setup | IT or buddy |
| +2 hours | Team introductions | Team |
| Midday | Lunch or a social call | Manager and team |
| Afternoon | Company overview, values and how work gets done | Manager |
| Late afternoon | Role expectations and the 30-60-90 plan | Manager |

## First week

- [ ] Required training (security, privacy, conduct, role-specific)
- [ ] Key documents for the role
- [ ] One-to-one with each close teammate
- [ ] Shadow key meetings or customer conversations
- [ ] First small, real task
- [ ] End-of-week check-in with the manager

## 30, 60 and 90 day goals

Write two or three goals per stage. Each goal names the outcome and how both people will know it is done. For example, at 30 days: "Handles routine tickets alone with a quality score at the team average."

## Key contacts

| Person or team | For |
| --- | --- |
| Manager | Priorities and day-to-day questions |
| Buddy | Everyday questions and how things work |
| IT | Equipment and access |
| HR | Contract, benefits and policies |

## Tips

- Customize for the role and level.
- Do not overload day one.
- A buddy who is not the manager makes a big difference.
- Remote hires need extra care: scheduled social time and explicit written guides.
