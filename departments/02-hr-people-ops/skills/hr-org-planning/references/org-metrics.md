# Org structure rules of thumb and people metrics

These are common rules of thumb, not standards. Every organization differs; use them to start a conversation.

## Structure

| Measure | Often works | Worth a look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Span of control (direct reports per manager) | About 5 to 8 | Fewer than 3 or more than 12 |
| Team size | About 5 to 9 | Fewer than 4 or more than 12 |
| Individual contributors per manager | About 6 to 10 | Fewer than 4 (top-heavy) |
| Management layers | As few as the work allows | Decisions waiting on many approvals |
| Single points of failure | Every critical skill has a backup | One person holds a critical skill or access alone |

## Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headcount | People employed on a date | By team, level, location |
| Hires | People who joined in the period | |
| Exits | People who left in the period | Split voluntary and involuntary if known |
| Attrition rate | Exits in the period / average headcount in the period | Average headcount = (start + end) / 2 for a simple estimate |
| Average tenure | Mean time employed | Median is often more useful |
| Time to fill | Days from approval to accepted offer | From recruiting data |

Worked example: 40 people at the start of the quarter, 44 at the end, 3 exits. Average headcount = (40 + 44) / 2 = 42. Quarterly attrition = 3 / 42 = 7.1 percent.

## Privacy

- Use employee codes, never names.
- Suppress any group with fewer than five people.
- Diversity metrics only when the user asks and provides lawfully collected data, in aggregate, for monitoring.

## Text org chart format

```text
Head of Customer Experience
  Support Lead (3 reports)
    Support Specialist x3
  Success Manager x2
```
