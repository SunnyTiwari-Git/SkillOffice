# Review templates and language check

## Self-assessment

1. **Key accomplishments (3 to 5).** For each: situation, your contribution, impact (measurable where possible).
2. **Goals review.** Table: goal, status (met, exceeded, missed), evidence.
3. **Growth.** New skills, wider scope, leadership moments.
4. **Challenges.** What was hard and what you would do differently.
5. **Goals for next period.** Specific and measurable.
6. **Support you need from your manager.**

## Manager review

- **Period and role.**
- **Summary.** Two or three sentences on overall performance, grounded in the examples below.
- **Strengths.** Each with a specific example and its effect.
- **Development areas.** Each with a specific, actionable next step.
- **Goals table.** Goal, result, comment.
- **Contributions and impact.**
- **Development plan.** Skill, current level, target, actions, support.
- **Overall rating.** Entered by the manager, using the company's scale and definitions.

## Language check

| Look for | Example | Rewrite toward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vague praise or criticism | "Great job this year", "needs to improve communication" | "Cut average ticket response time from 9 to 4 hours by redesigning triage" |
| Personality labels | "abrasive", "lazy", "not a team player" | The observed behavior and its effect: "In three planning meetings interrupted teammates before they finished" |
| Commonly biased descriptors | "bossy", "emotional", "aggressive" (often applied unevenly by gender); "low energy", "not adaptable" (often age-coded) | Specific, observable behavior and impact |
| Protected or personal circumstances | Mentions of leave, pregnancy, health, family, religion, age | Remove; they are not performance evidence |
| Unsupported ratings | "Exceeds" with no examples | Add evidence or adjust the proposal for the manager to decide |

Focus on behavior and results, not personality. Balance strengths and development areas. Nothing in a review should be a surprise to the employee.
