# Finance and Accounting

Status: **pilot, not yet platform-tested.** Planned platforms: Claude Code and Antigravity.

> **Not professional advice.** This department prepares drafts and workpapers. It is not professional accounting, tax or investment advice. A qualified professional must review before anything is posted, paid, filed or reported.

This department helps founders, bookkeepers, accountants and finance teams with the routine work of keeping the books: drafting journal entries, reconciling accounts, running the month-end close, preparing statements and management commentary, forecasting cash, chasing receivables and reviewing bills, preparing for audits, and organizing documents for tax season.

It works only from files you provide. It shows its calculations so you can check them, uses your currency and date format, and never invents a number. It never posts entries, pays bills, sends reminders or files anything: those actions stay with you.

## How to use it

Ask the department manager, `fin-manager`, in plain words. For example:

- "Close October. Here are the bank statement and trial balance."
- "Why doesn't our bank reconciliation agree?"
- "Prepare the March management pack with commentary on the budget variances."
- "Can we make payroll on the 15th?"
- "Who owes us money, and which bills should we pay this week?"
- "Prepare the controls testing workpapers for purchase to pay."
- "Get our year-end tax pack ready for the accountant."

The manager plans which skills to run, skips the ones you do not need, does one round of fixes and gives you one report.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| fin-journal-entries | Drafts balanced journal entries with calculation support, for review before posting |
| fin-reconciliation | Reconciles bank, ledger, settlement and intercompany balances and flags what does not agree |
| fin-month-end-close | Plans and tracks the close, runs the sign-off step and writes a plain-English month summary |
| fin-financial-statements | Drafts the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement with tie-out checks |
| fin-variance-analysis | Explains budget and period variances with drivers that add up exactly |
| fin-cash-flow | Forecasts cash for 13 weeks or 30/60/90 days with low, expected and high cases |
| fin-invoicing-collections | Drafts payment reminders and reviews bills, then proposes a payment run for your approval |
| fin-audit-prep | Prepares controls testing workpapers and auditor request lists |
| fin-tax-prep-organizer | Organizes a tax pack and questions for your tax adviser; no tax advice |

## Playbooks

| Playbook | Skills, in order |
| --- | --- |
| Month-end close | Close plan, journal entries, reconciliation, sign-off and month summary |
| Reconciliation review | Reconciliation, correcting entry drafts |
| Management report | Financial statements, variance analysis |
| Cash-flow forecast | Cash flow, collections (if a shortfall needs levers) |
| Audit preparation | Audit prep, reconciliation of key accounts |
| Tax season organizing | Verify books closed, tax pack |

## What it will not do

- Post, approve or upload entries; pay or schedule payments; send reminders; file or submit returns.
- Give tax, accounting or investment advice, or state tax rates and deadlines as fact.
- Put personal financial data, card numbers or tax identity numbers in reports or memory. Payroll appears as totals only.
