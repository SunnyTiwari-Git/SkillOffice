## Changes made from the upstream version

Modified by SkillOffice on 2026-11-12:

- Merged the topic and incident briefs with the meeting briefing and action item tracking into one brief skill.
- Removed the daily brief mode and every connector scan (email, calendar, chat, CLM, CRM, document stores), the suggestion of commercial legal research platforms, and the follow-up steps that distribute items or update systems. The skill works only from material the user provides.
- Kept and reworded: the brief sections, meeting-type preparation needs, incident priorities (preserve evidence, involve counsel, flag short notification deadlines), and action item rules (one owner, a date, a type).
- Added: the not-legal-advice notice, no assumptions about people or motives, no invented laws or outcomes, confidentiality marking, counsel attendance for regulator and dispute meetings, never sending or scheduling, no matter details in memory, the managed-run section and the handoff.
