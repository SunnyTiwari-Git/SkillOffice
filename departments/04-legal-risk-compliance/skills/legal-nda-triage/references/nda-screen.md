# NDA screening criteria and defaults

Use the company's own NDA standards when they exist. Otherwise use these common market defaults and say that defaults were used. Anything that depends on local law is marked "confirm with counsel".

## Defaults when no company standard exists

- Mutual obligations, unless only one side discloses.
- Agreement term of one to three years; confidentiality surviving two to five years after disclosure or termination; longer only for genuine trade secrets.
- All five standard carve-outs present (below).
- No non-compete, non-solicit, exclusivity or standstill.
- No residuals clause, or one limited to general skills retained in unaided memory, excluding trade secrets and patentable information.
- No licence or assignment of intellectual property.
- Governing law in an established commercial jurisdiction connected to the parties.

## Screening checklist

| Area | Pass when |
| --- | --- |
| Structure | Mutual or one-way in the right direction for the purpose; a standalone NDA, not confidentiality terms buried in a commercial deal |
| Definition | Limited to non-public information shared for the stated purpose; workable marking rules if marking is required; does not sweep in public or independently developed information |
| Obligations | Reasonable care (at least the care used for one's own confidential information); use limited to the purpose; disclosure limited to people who need to know and are bound by similar duties; no impractical requirements |
| Standard carve-outs | Public through no fault of the recipient; already known to the recipient; independently developed without using the information; received from a third party without restriction; disclosure required by law or court order, with notice where allowed |
| Permitted disclosures | Employees, contractors and professional advisers under similar duties; affiliates if the purpose needs it; regulators and courts when required |
| Term | Reasonable agreement term and survival period; not perpetual except for trade secrets |
| Return and destruction | On request or at the end; exception for copies kept by law, regulation or routine backups; a simple confirmation rather than a sworn statement |
| Remedies | Equitable relief acknowledged; no pre-set damages or penalties; symmetrical in a mutual NDA |
| Problem provisions | None of: non-solicit, non-compete, exclusivity, standstill (outside acquisition talks), broad residuals, IP licence or assignment, audit rights |
| Governing law | Established jurisdiction; law and venue consistent; no unexpected mandatory arbitration |

## Classification

| Result | When | Suggested route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green | Every row passes | Sign under the company's normal authority |
| Yellow | Minor deviations fixable in one pass | Counsel reviews the listed points |
| Red | Any serious deviation, or not really an NDA | Do not sign as is; full review or counter-propose the company's standard form |

## Common fixes

- **Overbroad definition:** limit to information marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential in the circumstances.
- **Missing independent development carve-out:** add it; without it, the company's own later work could be claimed as derived from the other side's information.
- **Non-solicit in an NDA:** delete it; if insisted on, limit to active targeting of named staff for about 12 months, not general hiring.
- **Broad residuals clause:** delete, or limit to unaided memory and exclude trade secrets, patentable information and any licence.
- **Perpetual confidentiality:** replace with a fixed period, with longer protection only for trade secrets.
