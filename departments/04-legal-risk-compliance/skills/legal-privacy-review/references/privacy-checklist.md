# Privacy review checklist

Common themes across modern privacy laws. Each law words and weights them differently; confirm specifics with counsel for the jurisdictions in scope.

| Theme | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose and basis | Is each use of personal data tied to a clear purpose? What is the legal ground (consent, contract, legal duty, legitimate interest or the equivalent in the applicable law)? Is consent, where relied on, specific, informed, freely given, recorded and as easy to withdraw as to give? |
| Transparency | Does a notice tell people what is collected, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, their rights and how to contact the company, at or before collection, in plain language and in the languages of the audience? |
| Minimization | Is every field needed for the stated purpose? Are optional fields marked optional? |
| Sensitive data | Health, financial, biometric, children's, government identifier or precise location data: is there a stronger basis, extra safeguards and restricted access? |
| Retention and deletion | Is there a retention period per data category, and is deletion actually carried out, including in backups and vendor systems? |
| Processors and sharing | Is there a written processing agreement with every vendor that handles personal data for the company? Are sub-processors disclosed with notice of changes? Is any sharing a "sale" or "sharing" under local law, with an opt-out where required? |
| Cross-border transfers | Which countries does data go to, and what transfer mechanism or permission applies under each relevant law? |
| Individuals' rights | Can the company find, export, correct and delete a person's data, and handle requests within the legal time limits? Who handles requests, and how is identity verified? |
| Security | Access control, encryption, logging, vendor security, staff training proportionate to the data. |
| Breach readiness | Is there a written incident plan, a way to decide quickly whether notification to authorities or individuals is required, and contacts for counsel? |
| Children | Are children's data and age assurance handled under the stricter rules that often apply? |
| Automated decisions | Are there decisions with significant effects made by automated means or profiling, with a way for people to get human review? |
| Accountability | Records of processing, impact assessments where required, a named owner such as a privacy or data protection officer, staff training. |

## When an impact assessment is usually expected

Large-scale processing of sensitive data, systematic monitoring of people, new technologies with privacy effects, profiling or automated decisions with significant effects, combining datasets in unexpected ways, and processing children's data at scale. Treat this as a prompt to ask counsel, not a legal test.
