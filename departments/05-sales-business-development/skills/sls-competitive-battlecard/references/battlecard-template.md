# Battlecard template

One page per competitor. If sellers cannot find the answer during a call, cut until they can.

## [Competitor name]

Last verified: [date]. Changes since the previous version: [what changed].

1. **When you will see them**: segments, deal sizes and triggers where they show up.
2. **Why buyers consider them**: their real appeal, in the buyer's words, with sources.
3. **Where they are strong**: honest, so sellers are not caught off guard.
4. **Where we win, and for whom**: tied to evidence (win notes, customer quotes). Unproven points are labeled as hypotheses.
5. **Discovery questions**: 3 to 5 questions that bring out the needs where the company is stronger, without criticizing the competitor.
6. **Top objections**: each with a one-line response and its proof point.
7. **When to walk away**: deal profiles where they are the better fit.
8. **Sources**: each fact with its link or document and date.

## Keeping it current

- Re-check pricing and packaging every quarter, and whenever sellers hear something new in a deal.
- Tag every competitor fact with its source and date so stale lines can be found and fixed.
- Assign an owner for each card.
