# Deal signals and probability adjustments

Start from the recorded probability, or the stage default if none is recorded. Apply each adjustment that the evidence supports, cite the evidence, and keep the result between 5 and 95 percent.

| Signal | Adjustment |
| --- | --- |
| Champion actively engaged (email or meeting in the last 14 days) | +10% |
| Multi-threaded (3 or more contacts with recent activity) | +5% |
| Executive sponsor identified and met | +10% |
| Mutual close plan agreed with the customer | +10% |
| No activity in 14 or more days | -10% |
| Champion silent for 14 or more days | -15% |
| New stakeholder introduced late in the cycle | -5% |
| Competitor actively in the deal | -10% |
| Close date moved out 2 or more times | -10% |
| Single-threaded | -10% |
| Open pricing gap between the customer's ask and the company's stated position | -10% |
| Open dispute, held invoice or service-level breach on the account | -10% |

When the company has its own win and loss history, its patterns may replace these weights. Say when they do.

## Stage reality tags

- **Matches the evidence**: the exit criteria for the stage are met.
- **Ahead of the evidence**: the record claims more progress than the evidence shows.
- **Behind the evidence**: the deal has progressed further than the record shows.

## What blocks advancement

Flag only qualification gaps that block the next step. A missing champion blocks. An unconfirmed budget number during early discovery may not. Items already in motion are listed with their expected landing date so the seller does not ask for them again.
