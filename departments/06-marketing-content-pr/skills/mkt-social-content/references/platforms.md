# Platform reference

Platform rules and limits change often. Treat these as starting points and check the platform's current help pages before publishing.

| Platform | Best for | Typical frequency | Strong formats |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | B2B, thought leadership, hiring | 3-5 per week | Text posts with a story, document carousels |
| X (Twitter) | Tech, real-time conversation, community | Daily or more | Threads, short opinions, replies |
| Instagram | Visual brands, lifestyle, local business | 3-7 posts per week plus Stories | Reels, carousels |
| TikTok | Awareness, younger audiences | Several per week | Short vertical video |
| Facebook | Communities, local businesses | 3-7 per week | Groups, native video |
| YouTube Shorts | Searchable short video | Several per week | Tutorials, quick tips with searchable titles |

## Hook shapes

- Curiosity: "I was wrong about {common belief}."
- Story: "Last week, {unexpected thing} happened."
- Value: "How to {outcome} without {pain}:"
- Contrarian: "{Common advice} cost us {specific result}."

Fill every shape with a real, specific detail.

## Repurposing map

| Source | Outputs |
| --- | --- |
| Blog post | LinkedIn insight post, carousel of key points, thread, short video summary |
| Podcast or video | 3-5 short clips, 1-2 text posts, a thread, a carousel, a newsletter section |
| Webinar | Slide carousel, Q&A thread, quote graphics, short clips |
| Newsletter | Main insight post, curated thread, stat graphic |

## Short video structures

- Problem-solution (15-30 s): hook with the problem (0-3 s), why it matters, the fix, the CTA.
- List (30-60 s): "X things that...", one item every 5-8 seconds, CTA.
- Tutorial (30-60 s): show the end result first, then the steps, then result and CTA.

Captions: at most 2 lines on screen, 3-5 words per line, timed to speech.

## Carousel basics

Slide 1 is the hook. One idea per slide. Large readable text. Last slide gives the takeaway and one CTA. Keep a consistent visual template from the brand guide.

## Metrics

- Awareness: impressions, reach, follower growth.
- Engagement: engagement rate, comments, shares, saves.
- Conversion: link clicks, profile visits, messages, attributed leads.

If engagement is low, test new hooks, times and formats and engage more with others. If reach drops, try native formats and keep external links out of the post body where the platform penalizes them.
