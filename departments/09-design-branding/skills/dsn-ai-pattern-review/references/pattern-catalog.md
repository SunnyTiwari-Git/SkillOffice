# Generic pattern catalog

Signals that a design is running on defaults. Each can be the right choice for a specific brief; flag it only when it was not chosen for a reason. These are quality indicators, never evidence of who or what made the design.

## Layout

- The stock landing page: centered hero with headline, subheadline and two buttons, then a row of three identical feature cards, then logos, testimonials, pricing and a final call to action, in that order, regardless of the business.
- Everything centered, at every width, with no grid or alignment logic.
- Sections that all have the same height, padding and structure, so nothing leads.
- Bento grids of mixed tiles used as decoration rather than to show related information.

## Color and effects

- Purple-to-blue or pink-to-orange gradient washes as backgrounds, buttons or text, unrelated to the brand.
- Glowing blurred color blobs behind the hero.
- Glassmorphism (frosted panels) on everything.
- A palette that ignores `brand.colors`.
- Near-black tinted backgrounds and one neon accent used as a default "modern" look.

## Typography

- One word in the headline highlighted with a gradient, italic or a different color.
- Small all-caps labels above every section heading.
- Headlines built from vague power words ("Unlock", "Supercharge", "Elevate", "Seamless").
- Default system or ubiquitous fonts when the brand specifies others.

## Icons and imagery

- Emoji used as feature icons or bullet points.
- Mixed icon styles (outline and filled, different stroke widths).
- Generic stock or generated illustrations (floating people, abstract 3D shapes) with no link to the product.
- Placeholder-like dashboard screenshots that do not show the real product.

## Copy

- Filler copy: "Lorem ipsum", "Your trusted partner", "Take your business to the next level", "Built for the future".
- Feature lists with three items of identical length and rhythm.
- Unverifiable claims ("loved by thousands", "best-in-class") with no source.
- Every link and button ending with an arrow.

## Components and motion

- One corner radius and one soft grey shadow on every element regardless of hierarchy.
- Cards inside cards inside cards.
- Fade-and-slide-up entrance animation on every section; hover lift on every card.
- Numbered markers (01, 02, 03) on content that is not a sequence.
- Middle dots or slashes used as decorative separators everywhere.

## What distinctive looks like instead

- Choices traceable to the subject: materials, vocabulary, imagery and data from the company's own world.
- Real content: actual product screens, real photos, specific numbers with sources.
- One bold element, with everything else calm.
- Hierarchy shown through deliberate differences in size, weight, spacing and radius.
