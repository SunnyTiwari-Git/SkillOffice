## Agile variant

For agile teams, replace the work breakdown with an ordered backlog of epics and stories, the schedule with a sprint or release outline, and milestones with release goals. Keep the RACI, assumptions and governance sections.

## Worked example (illustrative only)

A two-person team plans a help center relaunch. Likely effort: content audit 4 days, information architecture 3 days, writing 10 days, review 3 days, publishing 2 days, for 22 working days of effort. With each person available for about 60 percent of their week to this project, two people supply about 6 effective days a week, so the work takes about 4 weeks. Adding a 3-day buffer before the review milestone gives a draft of about 4.5 weeks, pending confirmation of availability.
