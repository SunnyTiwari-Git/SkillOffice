# RAID log template and rating guide

## Risk rating

| Likelihood \ Impact | Low | Medium | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | Medium | High | Critical |
| Medium | Low | Medium | High |
| Low | Low | Low | Medium |

Impact guide: high means a milestone slips, the budget is exceeded or a client commitment is at risk; medium means extra effort absorbed within the plan's buffer; low means minor inconvenience.
