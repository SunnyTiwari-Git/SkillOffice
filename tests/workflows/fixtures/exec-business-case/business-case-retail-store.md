# Business case: first retail store (fictional, for testing)

## Proposal
Open a 2,000 sq ft flagship store in downtown Seattle in Q2 2027.

## Numbers
- Fit-out cost: $250,000
- Rent: $9,000 per month
- Staff: 3 people
- Revenue: the store will sell $2,000,000 in year one because our online sales are $4,000,000 and half of customers prefer stores.
- Payback: 4 months.

## Risks
None significant.
