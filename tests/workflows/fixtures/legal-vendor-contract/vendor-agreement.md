# Master Services Agreement (fictional, for testing)

Between Northwind Running Co. ("Customer") and FastShip Logistics Inc. ("Vendor").

1. Term. This Agreement renews automatically for successive 3-year terms unless Customer gives notice 180 days before renewal.
2. Fees. Vendor may change fees at any time by posting new rates on its website.
3. Liability. Vendor's total liability is limited to $100. Customer's liability is unlimited.
4. Indemnity. Customer shall indemnify Vendor for any claim arising from the services, including Vendor's own negligence.
5. Data. Vendor may use Customer's customer names, addresses and order history for any purpose, including sale to third parties.
6. Termination. Vendor may terminate at any time without notice. Customer may terminate only for Vendor's material breach uncured after 120 days.
7. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of a jurisdiction to be chosen by Vendor at the time of any dispute.
